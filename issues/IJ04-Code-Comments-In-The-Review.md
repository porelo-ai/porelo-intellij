# IJ04 — Code comments in the review

**Status:** Implementing.
**Size:** L - SARIF 2.1.0 reading and writing in `Gate`, inline diff threads through [IJ12](README.md#ij12)'s adapter, a discussion panel in `ReviewEditor` for file, general and outdated threads, read-only fix previews, and source-level and risk-class badges; the `collaborationTools` adapter ([IJ12](README.md#ij12)) and held or outgoing forge releases ([IJ13](README.md#ij13)) are split out.

**What must be true.** A developer who reviews waiting work in IntelliJ comments and proposes line replacements on its diff, files, or review using IntelliJ's code-review gutter and inlays, and reads in the same place what checking and building agents, linters, and people on a forge said, all carried as SARIF 2.1.0 and marked with their authenticated source level, origin, and the push's risk class. The interface shows only the counts of open findings and threads per push and writes no comment, so the review tab is the one place where a comment is written, answered, resolved and read at its line, file or review.

## The shape

### 1. Comments, findings and suggestions as SARIF 2.1.0

- Every comment, agent or linter finding, reply, and line-replacement suggestion is read and sent through the daemon's comment calls on `Tasks1` only as SARIF 2.1.0 in Porelo's closed SARIF profile, read and written with `com.contrastsecurity:java-sarif` 2.0 and a maintained Jackson 2.x set hardened as Porelo's SARIF reader is. No custom `Anchor`, `Comment`, `Thread`, or `CommentOrigin` wire records exist beside SARIF.
- Root `version` is `"2.1.0"` and `properties["porelo/profileVersion"]` is `"1"`. Each run in `runs[]` carries `versionControlProvenance[0]` (`repositoryUri`, `revisionId`), `columnKind: "unicodeCodePoints"`, and `run.properties` (`"porelo/projectId"`, `"porelo/repositoryId"`, `"porelo/baseRevisionId"`, `"porelo/reviewRound"`, `"porelo/completeness"` in `complete`, `partial`, `failed` or `unknown`, and `"porelo/reviewVerdict"`).
- Each item in `runs[].results[]` carries a daemon UUIDv4 `guid`, `ruleId`, `kind` (`"review"` with `level: "none"` for comments, `"fail"` with `"note"`, `"warning"` or `"error"` for findings), `message.text`, optional `message.markdown`, `locations[0].physicalLocation.artifactLocation.uri`, `region` (`startLine`, `endLine`, `startColumn`, `endColumn`), and `result.properties` (`"porelo/anchorKind"` in `line`, `file` or `general`, `"porelo/diffSide"` in `old` or `new`, `"porelo/threadId"`, `"porelo/replyTo"`, `"porelo/threadState"` in `open` or `resolved`, `"porelo/sourceKind"`, `"porelo/sourceLevel"` in `E0`, `E1`, `I1` or `A`, `"porelo/authorId"`, `"porelo/authorDisplayName"`, `"porelo/taskId"`, `"porelo/taskRole"`, `"porelo/forgeInstanceId"`, `"porelo/outdated"`, `"porelo/outdatedReason"`, `"porelo/draft"`, and `"porelo/deleted"`).
- A line-replacement suggestion is carried in `result.fixes[0].artifactChanges[0].replacements[0]` (`deletedRegion`, `insertedContent.text`).

### 2. Diff threads, discussion panel and read-only fix previews

- In `ReviewEditor`, a line or a range of up to 100 inclusive lines on the `old` or `new` side of the diff takes a comment and an optional replacement suggestion, and existing `porelo/anchorKind: "line"` threads on the current push's commits are rendered beside their lines through [IJ12](README.md#ij12)'s replaceable adapter over `intellij.platform.collaborationTools`.
- File comments (`porelo/anchorKind: "file"`, without a `region`), general review comments (`porelo/anchorKind: "general"`, without `locations`), and outdated threads (`porelo/outdated: true` or anchored to an earlier commit or `porelo/reviewRound`, relocated or marked outdated by the daemon) are shown in a discussion panel in `ReviewEditor`.
- A suggestion (`result.fixes[0]`) is shown inside its comment as a read-only `SimpleDiffRequest` preview computed in memory over the cached blob. The plugin never applies a suggestion to a document or working tree: the agent applies it in its task, and the result returns through the gate as a new push.
- A reject in `Decisions.reject` drops the push with the person's reason while the daemon delivers the open SARIF threads to the task as structured review data.
- Releasing or dropping held inbound forge comments (`E0`) and outgoing forge comments, replies, suggestions, and verdicts is [IJ13](README.md#ij13)'s.

### 3. Security requirements

- **Hardened SARIF reading and writing:** Read and write SARIF with `com.contrastsecurity:java-sarif` 2.0 and a maintained Jackson 2.x BOM replacing its transitive Jackson 2.11.x. Enforce the stream limits of Porelo's hardened SARIF reader before allocating model objects, and the decoded bounds of Porelo's SARIF profile, taken from there and never copied with other values. Enable `STRICT_DUPLICATE_DETECTION`, `FAIL_ON_TRAILING_TOKENS`, and `FAIL_ON_UNKNOWN_PROPERTIES`; disable scalar/array coercions, numeric enum values, unknown enum fallbacks, and class-name polymorphism; validate against the closed profile allow-list before POJO binding; drop sender snippets, artifact contents, invocations, external references, and unlisted property bags; refuse Unicode bidi controls and zero-width characters; and construct fresh model objects when sending so untrusted object graphs are never round-tripped. Renew `gradle/verification-metadata.xml` ([IJ09](README.md#ij09)) when dependencies move.
- **Git-tree anchor resolution in the bare cache:** Validate `artifactLocation.uri` as a canonical repository-relative path (decode once, re-encode and compare, within the profile's length bounds; schemes, authority, absolute paths, backslashes, drive prefixes, queries, fragments, empty or dot components, encoded separators, decoded percent signs, and control or format characters refused) and resolve it only against pinned regular blobs in `ReviewCache` (`changes.commit()` matching `revisionId` for `new`, `changes.base()` matching `porelo/baseRevisionId` for `old`, where `ReviewCache.files` reads only `blob` objects from `git cat-file --batch` and ignores symlinks, trees, and submodules). Never fetch `$schema`, `hostedViewerUri`, `workItemUris`, or remote avatars, and never resolve a path against the host file system or an open IDE project.
- **Safe comment rendering:** Render only the permitted Markdown subset (the profile's: paragraphs, simple lists, emphasis, and code) with all network access disabled (no `AsyncHtmlImageLoader`, no remote or local images, no raw HTML, no external links, autolinks, or `jetbrains://` links). Render daemon-authenticated origin (`porelo/sourceKind`, `porelo/authorDisplayName`, `porelo/taskId`, `porelo/taskRole`) and source-level badges (`E0`, `E1`, `I1`, `A`, as the daemon stamps them) in header components outside the untrusted body.
- **Suggestion confinement:** Admit at most 1 `fix`, 1 `artifactChange`, and 1 `replacement` on the `new` side of a regular blob, whose `deletedRegion` equals the primary anchor and whose `insertedContent.text` stays within the profile's bounds for a fix. Outdated, `old`-side, or `E0` suggestions have no actionable control, and every fix preview is read-only with no local apply action.
- **State and authority separation:** A comment the person writes in the review tab is stamped `I1` by the daemon; resolving a thread here changes only what is shown, and its gate finding is cleared only by the person's confirmation in the daemon, while a forge thread resolved by an `I1` account clears its finding, as [IJ13](README.md#ij13) shows. Keep thread resolution (`porelo/threadState`) separate from `result.suppressions`, `run.properties["porelo/reviewVerdict"]`, and `Gate.approve`, which binds to the push's commit. Suppressions, `baselineState`, a thread resolved on a forge by a non-`I1` account, or missing results never clear a gate finding or authorize approval. Display `run.properties["porelo/completeness"]` (`complete`, `partial`, `failed`, `unknown`) explicitly in `ReviewEditor` so a partial or failed run never looks like a clean review. Show the push's daemon-stamped source level (`E0`–`A`), code risk class (`C0`–`C3`), and two-person approval state for `E0` and `C3` changes in `WaitingTree` and `ReviewEditor`.

## Plan

1. **Hardened SARIF profile reader and writer:** Use Porelo's hardened SARIF reader and writer if `porelo-wire` exports them, or add a `@NullMarked` package `org.fuin.porelo.intellij.sarif` (checked by `NullMarkedPackagesTest`) bundling `com.contrastsecurity:java-sarif` 2.0 and a maintained Jackson 2.x BOM in `gradle/libs.versions.toml` and `build.gradle.kts` and renewing `gradle/verification-metadata.xml` from an empty Gradle home ([IJ09](README.md#ij09)). Enforce the pre-binding stream limits, profile allow-list, canonical `artifactLocation.uri` validation, single-fix anchor confinement, and bidi/zero-width refusal before returning typed review data. Red first in unit tests against adversarial fixtures: an oversized document, nesting deeper than that reader allows, duplicate keys, unknown properties, a scheme or `..` in `artifactLocation.uri`, a bidi or zero-width character in `message.text`, and a fix outside its primary `new`-side anchor are each refused before binding.
2. **`Gate` (`org.fuin.porelo.intellij.gate.Gate`):** Add the `Comment`, paged `Comments`, and `ResolveThread` calls on `org.fuin.porelo.Tasks1` (`Gate.INTERFACE`), sending and reading validated SARIF 2.1.0 documents and cursor pages. Extend `PendingPush`, `Review`, `Waiting.Entry`, and `Gate.pending`, `Gate.review`, and `Gate.approve` to read the push's daemon-stamped source level (`E0`–`A`), code risk class (`C0`–`C3`), and two-person approval state, and map comment, anchor, fix, and approval refusals in `Gate.refusal` into `GateException` subclasses in words, as `MovedSinceReviewException` and `BranchExistsException` do today. Red first in `GateTest` against `VarlinkServer`: valid and refused SARIF comment calls, paged `Comments` preserving `porelo/completeness`, and refusal when `revisionId` or `porelo/baseRevisionId` disagrees with the push.
3. **Anchor and thread mapping against `ReviewCache` (`org.fuin.porelo.intellij.ide.ReviewTab`):** In `ReviewTab.read`, read the push's SARIF pages through `Gate` off the UI thread and keep them in `ReviewTab.Read`. Resolve each result's `artifactLocation.uri`, `region`, and `porelo/diffSide` as a pure function against the pinned blob bytes read from `ReviewCache.files` (`changes.base()` for `old`, `changes.commit()` for `new`, and `ReviewCache.file` for an earlier round's pinned commit), partitioning results into current inline `line` threads for each `ReviewTab.Side` and `file`, `general`, or `outdated` threads for the discussion panel without touching the host file system or any open IDE project. Red first in `ReviewTabTest`: a thread on an earlier commit or a missing/non-blob path is classified as outdated or unanchored rather than placed on the current diff, and an `old`-side or out-of-range fix is refused.
4. **`ReviewEditor`, `WaitingTree`, and `Decisions` (`org.fuin.porelo.intellij.ide`):**
   - In `WaitingTree.Renderer` and `ReviewEditor.header`, display the push's source level (`E0`–`A`), code risk class (`C0`–`C3`), SARIF `porelo/completeness`, and two-person approval state.
   - In `ReviewEditor.showSelected`, pass the selected file's inline threads and comment, reply, and resolve callbacks to [IJ12](README.md#ij12)'s `collaborationTools` adapter through the context tag on the `SimpleDiffRequest`, rendering comment bodies with the safe, network-free Markdown subset and rendering origin and `porelo/sourceLevel` badges in the header outside the body.
   - Add a discussion panel in `ReviewEditor` for `file`, `general`, and `outdated` threads, and render `result.fixes[0]` inside its comment as a read-only `SimpleDiffRequest` preview over the cached blob bytes with no apply action.
   - Keep `Decisions.approve` bound to `entry.push().commit()` and independent of `porelo/threadState`, `porelo/reviewVerdict`, and `suppressions`; `Decisions.reject` calls `gate.reject` as today while the daemon delivers the open SARIF threads with the reason.
5. **Live acceptance on the VM (`LiveGateTest`):** Against a real daemon that takes SARIF comments, write a line comment with a suggestion, a file comment, and a general comment, read them back as SARIF, reject the push so the task receives the open threads and replies, and verify the reply arrives with daemon-stamped origin `A`.

## Acceptance

- On a VM against a real daemon: a line comment, a file comment, and a general comment written in the review tab reach the task as SARIF 2.1.0; an anchor outside the pinned blob, a path with a scheme or `..`, a bidi or zero-width character, or a fix outside its primary `new`-side anchor is refused in words; the agent's reply appears in the thread with its daemon-stamped origin and source level. Each seen to fail first against today's plugin.
- A suggestion is shown as a read-only diff preview and the working tree of any project open in the IDE is unchanged afterwards.
- `run.properties["porelo/completeness"]`, the push's source level (`E0`–`A`), code risk class (`C0`–`C3`), and two-person approval state are shown, and resolving every thread or receiving a SARIF verdict or suppression never enables or performs approval on its own.
